# RFC: Shape 1.0 (decision record for model version 7)

**Status.** Proposed for 1.0, pending the roadmap's exit criteria. Supersedes the version 6 model. **Scope.** The decisions that turn version 6 into the 1.0 release candidate, each tied to the measurement or attack that forced it. **Sources.** Experiments H1–H6 and DRT (scratch `shape1work/H1` … `DRT`); build feedback from the kernel, prototype core, providers and dogfood agents (cited "build/kernel", "build/core #n", "build/providers", "build/dogfood"); upheld red-team gaps (`redteam/agent`, `redteam/lawyer`, cited by id); the completeness review of the version 7 draft (cited "completeness review"). Numbers are as reported by those runs. *Prediction* marks a decision whose effect was not measured; *analytical* marks one resting on a counterexample in the text rather than on a measurement.

## 1. Motivation

Shape keeps architectural intent legible while agents write most of the code. 0.x does this with path-level guards and syntax checks: every edit under a guarded path demands review, whether or not it touches the intent, and a loosening is a label someone remembers to apply. 1.0 replaces that with typed claims, evidence records and derived obligations, so that review effort goes where a claim, an observation or a loosening question says it should, and a deterministic checker can show why. Version 6 was a complete, implementable model; the experiments then measured it on Shape's own history, a Verus corpus, mutation suites and the 0.x loosening fixtures, and found it right in kind but wrong in several defaults. Version 7 fixes those defaults without adding a concept, and closes the gaps the completeness review found in the inputs the checker trusts.

## 2. The model in brief

Fifteen concepts (core §2), unchanged from version 6: Shape (with policy), Actor, Component (boundaries), Resource, Unit, Effect, Claim, Template, Exception, Note, Provider, Record, Manifest, Change, Obligation. Authors write claims about components, resources and units, bound to code identities that a manifest fingerprints per revision. Providers produce in-toto records: observations, tests, proofs, judgments, equivalences. One pure function, `derive`, compares a base with a head and returns a verdict, errors and four kinds of obligation: contradiction, unestablished, loosening, coverage. The checker decides structure, authority and conduct; providers decide laws; judgment decides informal laws. Governance is read from the base, so a change cannot loosen the rules it is judged by. Loosening is six fixed questions (L0, L1, L3, L4, L6, L7) with counterexamples. A Lean kernel proves the core; a TypeScript prototype implements it; the annex text is the reference both answer to.

## 4. Decisions

Each decision gives what changed, the evidence, and whether its effect is measured.

**D1. `forbid hatch *` expands to the declared hatch verbs plus `Undeclared`, never `Hatch`.** Undeclared registry kinds lift to the reserved verb `Undeclared ⇒ Hatch`. Version 6 included a clause on `Hatch` itself, whose down-set holds every declared kind, so `allows hatch` never took effect. Evidence: build/kernel and build/core #1 reproduce a preexisting sound contradiction at HostTrusted's allowed `external_body` in Example 2 (fp `dc8454f2…`; 3 obligations, not 2); H6: 9/9 kind clauses redundant under the catch-all; H5: the catch-all dropped `pass` verdicts from 60 to 0 and raised mean open obligations from 190 to 204. Measured (the defect); the fix gives Example 2's two obligations by construction.

**D2. Summaries are direct, and L1 decides them (L2 retired).** `effects complete` bounds the atoms of the unit's own code; effects reached through calls belong to the callee, and `a via v` states a dependency. A complete summary is a per-position upper bound, so it enters P and Lvl and L1 decides it. Summaries have no `call(L)` channel. Evidence: build/dogfood: transitive `complete` gave 337 sound effects.upper contradictions on Shape, 336 witnessed only through 1–9 call hops, and "only X may do V" needed except lists tracking the call graph; H1: 635 mechanical blocking contradictions, mostly effects.upper from 0.x direct summaries; H2: one call fanned out into 118 (S07) and 103 (S06) summary obligations; H3: 25/39 behaviour-preserving refactors kept an owed coverage obligation from the summary's `call(ts)` channel, the only thing blocking 10/11 out-of-Req dogfood bugs; build/core #2 and build/kernel: that channel changed Example 1 Variants A and C and Example 3 Step 1 (owed, not pass); H4: 6 of 29 forward L1 obligations, including the one wrong fixture (read_only_function_added), were worlds the head's own summary rejects. Cost: H2's 4 arm-A drifts caught only through transitive summaries (S09–S12) now rely on structure claims. Measured for the costs removed; the new classification of read_only_function_added is a prediction.

**D3. Coverage is asked only where something can observe, and observability is read from the base.** A (claim, channel) pair is needed at changed code only if the channel is observable or the claim's tier\* there is final; lower bounds need nothing; unregistered resource-code kinds have no carriers. Observability, which decides need, exploitation and the declared-only listing, reads the base's provider declarations alone; meeting a need still requires soundness in base and head. A checker-decided claim with no observable channel is declared-only by construction, listed with its route and counted per tier: this amends invariant 3 from "policy marks it declared-only" to "policy or the checker marks it". Evidence: H2: coverage fired on 7/7 behaviour-preserving controls and DefaultPolicy blocked 4 of them, exactly as it blocked 12 drift runs; build/dogfood: 75 of 82 bound codes had no observer and unregistered kinds defaulted to every carrier, giving blocking critical coverage in 5 of 6 scenarios, 4 of 8 replayed commits and adoption step 2; H1: a lower bound's footprint dragged unrelated `fs.path` channels onto pure helpers (sample 10); H6: 33 of 154 claims are observably vacuous, so the declared-only case is common and must be visible. The base-only need side is analytical (completeness review: with base ∧ head, a head dropping `absence` removed a blocking critical coverage obligation in the run, falsifying T2′); vector A.7.4.2. *Prediction*: the H2 controls raise no coverage.

**D4. Law obligations are introduced through the cones of the records that bear on them; guards see their helpers.** A law obligation is introduced iff Req, or the cone of a record that contradicts the claim or holds the obligation's reason, meets the code delta; a contradiction is not introduced when a usable base-revision record of the same provider made it with an equal counterexample (assertion and inputs). Cones close over calls and the manifest's `refs`. For judgments, Req of `on unit u` adds the functions of u's component that u references transitively, `this` and `super` methods and module object members included; identities without the cited projection are cited at `file`. Evidence: H3: V-new2 raised blocking from 33 and 29 of 43 to 43/43 in every arm and the dogfood from 13/24 to 24/24, with 0 new false obligations on 39 + 12 refactors; H3 without re-runs: the unit arms blocked 33 and 31 of 43 at critical against 43/43 on components, the misses being callee bugs whose stale obligation was preexisting, which the reason-holding cone now introduces (completeness review); H2: dependency-routed drift (L04, L06–L08) produced the right contradiction but preexisting in 4/4; redteam agent/D7 (partial) and agent/E1 (succeeded); H3 D10 and H2 C04: refactors re-introduced 2 blocking contradictions of laws already failing at base, which the counterexample clause removes without silencing a new failure. Measured (H3) for introducedness; the reason-holding cone, `this.m` resolution and the counterexample clause are predictions.

**D5. A structure contradiction is introduced only by a new lifted edge.** Evidence: H2: newness per fact flipped 41 old cycle obligations to introduced in S06, and blocked control C13 (a new test file adding a call along an existing component edge). *Prediction*: C13 passes; S06 keeps its old obligations preexisting.

**D6. Hatch facts at edited code are new; moved code is relocated, not new.** `own` replaces nested identities by path-free names and resolves nothing, so a moved unchanged identity keeps its own digest; a new identity uniquely matching a deleted one by kind and own digest is relocated, is no finest change, and compares its observed and hatch facts with the deleted one's. A hatch fact is new unless its identity is not a finest change and the base lists as many markers there or at the relocation source. Evidence: H5: recall over all E9 trust events rises from 66.6% to 87.8% (100% excluding removal-only commits) at precision 92.8% → 77.9% under the frozen surface; 477 of 493 trusted-body edits outside boundaries were silent; one directory move (anvil a27a5959, 0 E9 events) raised 291 blocking contradictions in 284 groups, and renames caused 32 of 55 false-positive commits; completeness review: the version 7 draft's annex defined `own` by identities, which include the path, so relocation could never match. Measured for the first half; relocation is a *prediction*.

**D7. A refutation is introduced when its footprint changed or the base had seen the fact.** Evidence: H2 arm X: all 7 removed calls were refuted soundly but stayed preexisting and owed. *Prediction*: 7/7 introduced in arm X.

**D8. The code delta is per (identity, projection), and file digests ignore formatting.** Evidence: H3: a move at the `file` projection alone met a Req entry at `own`, and an inert comment introduced an informal-law obligation (the comment scanner did not re-scan template literals); H2 C02: a formatting-only edit moved `ts.file.v1` and reopened RuleEngineDocumented. Measured (defect cases).

**D9. Producer change is narrow and cannot mask anything.** Per record the reasons are checked stale, expired, assumption, weak, producer-changed; per law assumption, weak, producer-changed, expired, stale. Producer change is reported only on records that would otherwise be usable and carry force, never on inconclusive records or for providers the base does not declare. A producer-changed answer accepts the producer change only; the law is re-evaluated. A moved harness digest is stale. Implementations may bind identities (`pkgjson("package.json#scripts.test")`). Evidence: H1: 494 of 851 obligations were producer-changed, from 11 commits touching package.json, bun.lock or tsconfig.json, 304 of them on inconclusive placeholders, 13/13 sampled false; agent/G7 and agent/G8 (succeeded), lawyer/L02 (succeeded, also at final), lawyer/L03 (succeeded), lawyer/L26 (partial). *Prediction*: H1's producer-changed count falls below 50.

**D10. The budget unit is the group, and groups join obligations by cause.** Groups also join obligations sharing a changed implementation or harness identity, or a removed or changed base item. Evidence: H1: only the group count halved (215, ratio 0.31, CI 0.24–0.41), but on the 22 cleanly elaborating heads groups gave 0.73, so grouping alone does not reach the target; one package.json script edit raised 37–48 obligations, one removed resource 24 L1 groups. Keys stay per claim (T7); keying producer change per provider (H1's proposal, 494 → 11) is rejected in favour of grouping, which buys the same budget without changing keys. Whether D2, D3 and D9 together bring the clean subset to ≤ 0.5 is a *prediction*, re-registered as H1′.

**D11. Final means final, without deadlock.** Rule 2 blocks *introduced* final sound contradictions; preexisting final drift is owed, never debt, and listed. tier\* for effect, summary and authority contradictions is the higher of the head tier and the base protection level at the fact's base position; a claim whose final loosening two persons answered contributes its head tier. Authority protects ungranted atoms in Lvl, so moving code from a final component to a lower one is an L1 loosening. Claim judgments, producer-changed answers, heuristic dismissals and coverage on final claims need two distinct persons and no agent; for coverage the two must cover each unmet final claim's owner, the component's base owner and the base shape owner. A final law is never satisfied through any hatch fact in its cone. Evidence: lawyer/L05, L04, L06, L08 (all succeeded); DRT (the version 6 witness condition for tier\* was implemented by neither checker); build/core #14; the coverage rule is analytical (completeness review: claim owner = component owner = a one-person team let one person lift final coverage), vector A.6.3.4.

**D12. Eligibility cannot be manufactured by the head or lost by authorship elsewhere.** The self fallback reads the base judges only; authorship excludes acts of this change, not persisted judgments of earlier ones; approve lines CI supplies without a relay make their authors eligible for this run (listed unauthenticated); when an item's owner is the shape owner, the two final persons are two persons of that actor. Evidence: lawyer/L01 (succeeded: narrowing judges in the head turned on the self fallback, violating T2′); build/core #12, #13; build/core #8 and build/dogfood (single-maintainer projects cannot use final, by design).

**D13. Ordered template parameters take integers; AppendOnly forbids Update; hosted heirs need equal closures.** Evidence: agent/B2 and agent/W2 (succeeded: lexicographic order let a test of `"1000ms"` establish a `"250ms"` law); agent/E6 (succeeded: `UPDATE … SET payload = NULL` rewrote an append-only log); agent/W6 (succeeded: both implementations ignored closure digests). The Update choice is decided; a redaction-tolerant template is deferred.

**D14. An empty Req makes a law vacuous, never attributed.** Evidence: lawyer/L19 (partial); H5: ReviewedSurface over an empty marked set kept a permanent `missing` obligation in the prototype while the kernel found it satisfied (18/18 sampled cases differ by exactly this).

**D15. A fact without `n` is new; presence-sound observers must report `n`.** Evidence: agent/C7 (partial: a base record without `n` masked a second violation in the same function).

**D16. Audit mode reads the head as governance base and treats everything as preexisting.** Evidence: build/core #3 (with no base, every provider was head-only, so Example 1's base could not pass); build/dogfood: 365 of 414 audit obligations blocked.

**D17. L1 parts are exact and counterexamples lie in the head; the order class is output.** An authority part needs an atom newly in both A and P; a counterexample is the least fact or (position, atom) the head admits, printed as the fact; one reverse pass gives the order class; an undecided rename suggests `was`. Evidence: build/kernel and build/core #11; H4: 8 of 31 printed counterexamples were worlds the head rejects; the reverse pass classified 30 of 33 cases correctly; one `was` line decided all 4 rename fixtures.

**D18. L7 reports inherited fields once and skips what an empty base cannot say.** Evidence: build/dogfood: one owner move produced 558 L7 items (351 claims, 101 units, 79 resources, 23 components, the shape); build/core #7: an empty base made every first adoption an undecided `shape` loosening that DefaultPolicy blocks. Actors gain `was`.

**D19. Migration mints nothing 0.x did not enforce, and drops nothing silently.** `owns` becomes the resource's owner, not std::Owns; 0.x `effects complete` maps to direct summaries; a guardless RefactorSensitive marker maps to its note; `origin: generated_ast` documents are mapper output; 0.x `unsafe` (summary metadata) becomes a decision note; the 0.x prelude AppendOnly (final HardDelete, Truncate, DropStorage) becomes std `NoHardDelete`, not std AppendOnly; `forbid final` becomes tier critical where the migrated owners lack two persons; shape traits, `description`, `require_context`, rationale guards, free and untargeted effect names, custom relation kinds, vendored packs, memory fields and the 0.x CLI each get a mapping or a reported drop (A.11.1). Evidence: build/core #4 and build/dogfood: Owns minted 47 rules causing 35 of 43 baseline errors (17 on SemanticDiagnostics), while the 0.x glossary says `owns` "does not gate grants and is not exclusive"; H1's 635 migration contradictions (D2); H4: a 0.x change removing only a guard vanished at migration; build/core #5: without the generated-AST rule, 436 E-unbound, 127 E-overlap and 4 E-template; 0.x sources: `unsafe` "changes nothing else" (docs effect model), the prelude AppendOnly's three final forbids and six shape traits (packages/shp-checker/src/prelude.ts), 8 shape-trait or `description required` uses in Shape's own `shape/`; build/dogfood: one-maintainer projects cannot hold final (E-policy). The construct mappings beyond Owns and summaries are analytical (completeness review).

**D20. Hatch completeness and kinds follow the code that exists.** An identity invoking, in any position (item, statement, expression, attribute or derive), a macro outside the registry's builtin list and not defined in the tree is not hatch-complete; `include!` counts only with a literal in-tree path to a hatch-complete file; std declares `unsafe` in rust and `ts_expect_error`, `ts_nocheck` in ts; Verus files keep vprobe's kinds. Evidence: lawyer/L36 (partial: a dependency macro hid an assume) and its residual (completeness review: attribute proc macros and `include!` of generated code); H5: the prototype scanner's macro rule raised coverage on 383 commits with no trust event (Vest precision 11.4%) and its plain-Rust `unsafe` counting added 3,172 markers; the vprobe port matched on 16,611/16,612 blobs; build/dogfood: 2 `@ts-expect-error` and 17 `@ts-nocheck` were invisible. *Prediction* for the builtin-list precision.

**D21. The provider contract says what a failure, a supply and an injected call are.** Deterministic test failures are `contradicts` with a counterexample; `inconclusive` records satisfy E-provider-missing; effects through a supplied root are reported at the supplier; unresolved calls through injected objects make the entry incomplete; `ts.call` invocation forms are fixed; Lean axioms map to tool, hatch or named assumptions; RJ06 rejections are counted in health. Evidence: H3: the stock mapping to `inconclusive` let 43/43 test-killed bugs merge as owed at normal, and without re-runs revision-tied providers blocked 39/39 refactors through E-provider-missing; build/dogfood: 52 E-provider-missing errors before inconclusive records, 3 after; 29 calls unresolved through an injected services object hid a real `Date.now()` drift; build/providers (supplier attribution, invocation forms, Lean mapping, RJ06 flipping blocked to pass).

**D22. Ambiguities found by DRT and the red team are settled in the annex.** Error subjects (N1, 2,495 cases), the loosening claim field (N2, 964), E-exception (N3, 324), witness versus support (NW, 74), command code (A4, 685), debt owner (A6, 385), coverage by ancestor entry (A8, 189), and A5, A7, A9–A16 (A.15); coverage bindings carry their (claim, channel) pairs (lawyer/L41); representatives sort sound and declared first, base unit overlaps take the strictest view, the per-item coverage answer rule wins, and a debt row lists every matching exception and takes its owner and expiry from the earliest-expiring one (lawyer/L38 (1), (2), (5), (6); build/core #9); L38 (3) equal-fp merging and (4) channel intersection with T(c) are kernel fixes in M2; a W_decl subject is not repeated as a contradiction, and exception-answered obligations appear in both `obligations` and `answered` (build/kernel).

**D23. The annex is the reference; a zero-residual differential gate guards both checkers.** The kernel computes cells, rejects out-of-fragment input with a reason, and serves as the oracle on the gate's cases; repository-scale use waits for performance. Evidence: DRT: as built, 1,505 cases (37.6%) carried kernel bugs and 1,263 (31.6%) prototype bugs; 4 shared deviations were invisible to differential testing; the bridge dropped the universe cell (HB1, 9 H4 disagreements); `via` and ρ were undocumented gaps (70 corpus cases; the 16 rename fixtures); the kernel took 1.04 s mean and 42.5 s max per corpus case, 2,826 s on 71 H5 commits against 4.9 s, and more than 3,600 s on a dogfood case the prototype checks in 5–6 s.

**D24. Teeth is relative to observability, and ungoverned code is a gap class.** Evidence: H6: 33 of 154 assessed claims are observably vacuous and only 337 of 111,130 W1 mutants (0.30%) are reportable with deployed providers; 2,049 of 3,679 identities are ungoverned, where a seeded clock read raised nothing; P1 is caught by L1 in change mode (G2 237/237) but 0/5,607 in audit; all 87 dogfood laws were unassessed for lack of `bound.mutants`; the 7 retired guards rest on laws killing 33/75 mutants.

**D25. Three small surface forms.** `except` globs on component paths (build/dogfood: a test file under a component's directory closed a sound, blocking false `calls` cycle that a more specific component could not reclaim); gha job identities (build/dogfood: multi-step CI jobs could bind only one step, and two genuine E-overlaps needed step rebinds); `was` on actors (D18).

**D26. What else stays.** Every critic decision of rounds 1–3 (139, mapped in experiments/shape-1/spec/critic-mapping-v6.json) remains in force except where D1 (overrides IMP3-07's "Hatch itself included"), D2 (EM2-3/EM3-6 hatch and summary elaboration, the L2 parts of S6 and IMP-10), D3 (EM3-2's "nothing omitted" in A.7.4.2) and D11 (version 4's rule that preexisting obligations block at tier final) override them.

**D27. The manifest is a complete, authenticated contract.** Manifest entries carry `executable` and `hatchComplete`, which decide channels and hatch coverage and which the checker cannot recompute; the shape may name a manifest identity, and when the base does, both manifests must be signed by it (else E-provider-missing); otherwise health lists them as unauthenticated. The kernel case `shape.kernel.case/v1` is the shared wire format, with manifests already authenticated. Evidence: H5: with vprobe-semantics manifests 0/585 growth commits were missed, with the prototype scanner 11/585, and the two agree on only 87.7% of marked blobs, while the scanner's completeness rule raised coverage on 383 trust-event-free commits, so whoever builds the manifest decides the hatch guarantees; build/kernel: the kernel input had no wire format, and both checkers used flags the contract did not define (completeness review); DRT HB1: a bridge-computed input (cells) silently changed 9 outcomes. Measured for the dependence; the identity rule is analytical.

**D28. A claim judgment is always a persisted record.** Without a relay, CI-supplied approve lines still act as answers for the run, but `shape judge` and `shape reject` lines make no judgment and are counted as `unrecorded`; judges sign their own judgment records instead. Evidence: H2: guard laws reported `missing` in arm A, which had no judgment records, and only arm X's 34 base-era signed judgments exercised the `stale` path; H1: with no judgment records, informal laws reported `missing` on every replayed commit; build/dogfood: with no relay declared, review lines acted only within the run, so nothing judged survived to the next run. Invariant 5 ("recorded as evidence with checkable citations") is otherwise violated by construction (completeness review). No acceptance test depends on unrecorded judgments.

## 5. In 1.0, deferred, cut

**Cut**: transitive summaries (D2); question L2 (D2); the `Hatch` catch-all clause (D1); coverage on unobservable channels and head-side observability (D3); producer change on non-establishing records (D9); exclusivity minted by migration (D19); judge lines acting without a record (D28).

## 6. Rejected alternatives

- *Keep transitive summaries and migrate 0.x `complete` as `partial`* (build/dogfood): reading them as partial still left 11 contradictions, lost 101 upper bounds and kept the call channel, the fan-out and the except-list problem.
- *Set the Hatch clause's except list to the union of allowing boundaries* (build/kernel): every boundary allowing one kind would then admit undeclared kinds too.
- *Key producer change by provider* (H1): it changes keys (T7) to buy what grouping by cause buys.
- *Keep coverage on every channel and drop DefaultPolicy's critical coverage block* (H2): it keeps the noise and removes the shell-purge case that justifies coverage (Example 1 B″).
- *Read need-side observability from base ∨ head*: a change that adds an absence-sound observer would raise needs it cannot meet (head-only providers never meet coverage), penalising the tightening.
- *Make unobservable final claims E-route, or require `evidence declared` on unobservable claims*: the first blocks first adoption, which lands providers after claims; the second adds a shape edit per claim (33 of 154 on Shape, H6) without changing what is checked. Final pairs keep coverage instead, and health lists the route.
- *Require receivers of supplied roots to be incomplete* (build/providers): infeasible for callback-heavy code.
- *Widen test Req to the unit's call cone*: providers would have to cite callees; record cones in introducedness give H3's result without that.
- *Introduce every contradiction of a law already failing at base whenever its Req changes*: it blocked refactors in H3 D10 and H2 C04; the equal-counterexample clause keeps new failures blocking.
- *Introduce unestablished obligations when the law was Sat at base*: the checker does not evaluate base laws; the reason-holding cone covers the measured case.
- *Keep the tier\* witness condition*: neither implementation applied it (DRT), and Lvl_b gives a definition both can.
- *Remove `include_str!` and `include_bytes!` from the builtin list*: they yield data, not code, and cannot hide markers.

## 7. Risks

- **Version 7's predictions are unmeasured.** D3, D4 (reason-holding cone, `this.m`, counterexample clause), D5, D6 (relocation), D7, D9 (producer counts), D10 (clean-subset groups) and D20 have mechanisms but no replay; the roadmap's M5 re-runs H1–H6 with criteria fixed in advance, some of which block release.
- **Failing laws guard less.** Under D4, a law already failing at base introduces a new contradiction only when its counterexample differs. A provider with random seeds turns every touch into a blocking contradiction; a deterministic test that stops at the first failure hides a second bug until the first is fixed. Shape's model has 2 such formatter laws; health lists laws contradicted at base.
- **Silence where nothing observes.** With D3, a call removal in a repository without an absence-sound call observer raises nothing, not even coverage (H2 arm A: 7 such drifts); health lists the relation as declared-only. H2's 4 call drifts caught through transitive summaries now need `relations complete`.
- **Hatch precision.** D6 costs about 15 points of precision on the frozen surface (92.8% → 77.9%); trusted functions edited often raise more blocking work.
- **Judgment staleness.** D4's Req closure stales a guard when a referenced helper changes; H1's budget may rise for informal laws. Measured in M5.
- **Observability and authentication as inputs.** D3 makes coverage depend on which providers the base declares, and D27 makes manifest authentication opt-in; a team can silence coverage by declaring no observer or skip the manifest identity.
