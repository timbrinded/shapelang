# Shape

Shape exists to keep architectural intent legible as software evolves: what
the system must preserve, how its parts may interact, and why those decisions
were made.

It gives people and agents a shared language of explicit, typed claims,
reviewed alongside code. A small, deterministic checker exposes contradictions
and unmet review obligations; evidence and judgment establish whether the
claims describe the implementation.
